    You’ve now used formulas and functions to calculate, summarize, and evaluate the account sales data. Use the example answer below to compare your work and check that your formulas return the expected results.

    Review your work

    Make sure that:

    • Total, average, highest, and lowest sales values are calculated correctly.
    • Your IF formula correctly identifies each account’s sales level.
    • Your IFERROR formula handles calculations that would otherwise return an error.
    • COUNTIF and SUMIF return the correct summary results.
    • Formulas have been applied consistently across the dataset.
    • Cell references update correctly when formulas are copied.

    If your results differ from the example, select the relevant cell and review the formula before making changes.
