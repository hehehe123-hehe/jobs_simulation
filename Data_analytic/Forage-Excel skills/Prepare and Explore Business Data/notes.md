Put Conditional Formatting Into Practice
Now it’s time to apply what you’ve learned. You’ll use the provided account sales dataset to practice identifying missing information, errors, and important values with conditional formatting.

Your task
Download and open the Account Sales Data for Analysis workbook. Take a few minutes to review the columns and become familiar with how the data is structured.

Then, apply conditional formatting to:

Highlight cells containing formula errors in purple with white text.
Highlight cells with missing values in yellow.
Highlight Product 2 cells for accounts that have not been cross-sold with Product 2 in orange.
Highlight 5-year CAGR values of 100% or higher in green.
Highlight negative 5-year CAGR values in red with white text.
Highlight accounts in the top 10% of 2021 unit sales in blue.
You may use Excel’s built-in conditional formatting rules or create your own rules where needed. Before submitting, review your workbook and make sure each rule is applied to the correct cells and range.
